Numbers, Dates & Telling Time

Use numbers in everyday situations, say your age, give dates, arrange meetings and tell the time in Italian.

60–75 minutesBeginner A1Grammar · vocabulary · dialogue · practice
Lesson map1. Learning goals2. Numbers from zero to twenty3. Tens and larger numbers4. Days, months and seasons5. How to say the date6. Telling the time7. Age, prices and quantities8. Dialogue: arranging a meeting9. Practice and answer key10. Speaking and writing assignment

PART 01

Learning goals

You will count, say prices and ages, name weekdays and months, ask the date and tell the time. Practise saying numbers aloud; recognition alone is not enough.

Combine tens and units: trentadue (32), quarantacinque (45), sessantotto (68). Remember the spelling changes before uno and otto.

Ho trentadue anni.
I am thirty-two years old.
Italian uses avere (to have) for age.

PART 05

How to say the date

Use Che giorno è oggi? to ask what day it is, and Quanti ne abbiamo oggi? or Qual è la data di oggi? to ask the date.

Oggi è martedì 12 maggio.
Today is Tuesday, 12 May.
Il mio compleanno è il 3 ottobre.
My birthday is on 3 October.

For the first day of the month, Italian commonly uses il primo: il primo maggio.

PART 06

Telling the time

Italian expressionMeaning
Che ore sono?What time is it?
È l'una.It is one o'clock.
Sono le due.It is two o'clock.
Sono le tre e un quarto.It is quarter past three.
Sono le quattro e mezza.It is half past four.
Sono le sei meno un quarto.It is quarter to six.
A mezzogiorno / a mezzanotteAt noon / at midnight

Use singular è l'una for one o'clock, but plural sono le… for other hours. For schedules, the 24-hour clock is common: alle 14:30.

PART 07

Age, prices and quantities

Age uses avere: ho vent'anni. Prices use costare: costa dieci euro (one item), costano cinque euro (plural). Numbers also appear in phone numbers, addresses and quantities.

Quanti anni hai? Ho venticinque anni.
How old are you? I am twenty-five.
Quanto costa? Costa dodici euro.
How much is it? It costs twelve euros.

PART 08

Dialogue: arranging a meeting

LucaCiao! Che giorno è oggi?
Hi! What day is it today?
MartaÈ giovedì, 14 marzo.
It's Thursday, 14 March.
LucaA che ora ci vediamo?
What time shall we meet?
MartaAlle sei e mezza, davanti al cinema.
At half past six, in front of the cinema.
LucaPerfetto. A dopo!
Perfect. See you later!

PART 09

Practice and answer key

  1. Write 28 in Italian.
  2. Translate: Wednesday.
  3. Say: It is one o'clock.
  4. Say: It is half past five.
  5. Translate: My birthday is on 8 June.
Answers

1. Ventotto. 2. Mercoledì. 3. È l'una. 4. Sono le cinque e mezza. 5. Il mio compleanno è l'8 giugno.

PART 10

Speaking and writing assignment

Say your age, birthday and today's date. Then arrange a meeting with a friend: choose a weekday, date, time and place. Write the exchange and read both roles aloud.

Ci vediamo sabato alle sette? Sì, a sabato!
Shall we meet on Saturday at seven? Yes, see you Saturday!

Lesson complete

Review the examples, say the Italian phrases aloud and complete the speaking task. Return to any section that still feels difficult. Regular short practice is more effective than memorizing a long list once.
