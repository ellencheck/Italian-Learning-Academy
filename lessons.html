YOUR ITALIAN JOURNEY · A1 TO C1

Learn Italian.
Step by step.

A structured path from your first introduction to confident, nuanced Italian. Each lesson is designed as a complete learning session—not a quick grammar note—with explanations, examples, vocabulary in context, guided practice, translation, reading, and a personal task.

How every full lesson works: objectives → teaching and pronunciation → grammar explained → vocabulary in context → dialogue or authentic text → guided examples → varied exercises → translation → comprehension → speaking/writing task → recap and progress checkpoint.

Academy standard: These are the course roadmaps. Each topic will be expanded into its own substantial lesson with explanations, worked examples, dialogue/text, vocabulary, several exercise types, translation, comprehension and a speaking or writing assignment. A level is completed through practice and review—not by reading a few sentences.
