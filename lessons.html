GRAMMAR PATH

Italian Lessons

Begin with the foundations. Each lesson has a short explanation, examples, and a completion button to save your progress.

A1 · Beginner

Lesson 1 — Greetings and introductions

Ciao! is informal and can mean “hello” or “goodbye.” Buongiorno means “good morning / good day.” Mi chiamo Alina. means “My name is Alina.” Piacere! means “Nice to meet you!”

Example: Buongiorno, mi chiamo Luca. Piacere!

Lesson 2 — Articles: il, la, un, una

Italian nouns have grammatical gender. Use il with many masculine singular nouns and la with many feminine singular nouns. The indefinite articles are un and una.

  • il libro — the book
  • la casa — the house
  • un ragazzo — a boy
  • una ragazza — a girl

Lesson 3 — Essere (to be)

Io sono — I am · Tu sei — you are · Lui/Lei è — he/she is · Noi siamo — we are · Voi siete — you are (plural) · Loro sono — they are.

Example: Io sono studentessa. Noi siamo amici.

Lesson 4 — Present tense: regular -are verbs

For regular -are verbs, remove -are and add: -o, -i, -a, -iamo, -ate, -ano.

parlare: parlo, parli, parla, parliamo, parlate, parlano.

Lesson 5 — Simple questions

Italian often forms questions through intonation. Tu parli italiano. (You speak Italian.) → Tu parli italiano? (Do you speak Italian?)
