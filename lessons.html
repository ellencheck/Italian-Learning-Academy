YOUR GRAMMAR PATH

Build your Italian, step by step.

Start with everyday expressions and essential grammar. Complete a lesson, then practice it in a real sentence.

01
FIRST CONVERSATIONS

Greetings & introductions

Ciao! is informal and can mean “hello” or “goodbye.” Buongiorno means “good morning / good day.” Mi chiamo Alina. — My name is Alina. Piacere! — Nice to meet you!

Buongiorno, mi chiamo Luca. Piacere!
02
NOUNS & ARTICLES

Il, la, un, una

Italian nouns have grammatical gender. Use il with many masculine nouns and la with many feminine nouns. Un and una are indefinite articles.

il librothe book
la casathe house
un ragazzoa boy
una ragazzaa girl
03
ESSENTIAL VERB

Essere · to be

Learn the forms of essere and use them to introduce yourself and describe people.

Io sono · Tu sei · Lui/Lei è
Noi siamo · Voi siete · Loro sono

Io sono studentessa. Noi siamo amici.

04
VERBS IN THE PRESENT

Regular -are verbs

Remove -are and add these endings: -o, -i, -a, -iamo, -ate, -ano.

parlare → parlo, parli, parla, parliamo, parlate, parlano
05
SPEAK WITH CONFIDENCE

Simple questions

Italian often forms questions with intonation. The word order can stay the same—the voice rises at the end.

Tu parli italiano. → Tu parli italiano?
You speak Italian. → Do you speak Italian?
