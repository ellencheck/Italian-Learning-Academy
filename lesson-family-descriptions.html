Family & Describing People

Talk about your family, relationships, age, appearance and personality using possessives and adjective agreement.

60–75 minutesBeginner A1Grammar · vocabulary · dialogue · practice

PART 01

Learning goals

Learn to introduce family members, say who people are, describe their appearance and personality, and use possessives correctly with family nouns.

PART 03

Talking about relationships

Use essere to identify someone and avere to say how many brothers or sisters you have.

Questa è mia sorella. Si chiama Giulia.
This is my sister. Her name is Giulia.
Ho due fratelli e una sorella.
I have two brothers and one sister.
Marco è il marito di Anna.
Marco is Anna's husband.

PART 04

Possessive adjectives

Possessives agree with the thing possessed, not with the owner. They usually come with an article: la mia casa, i tuoi amici.

OwnerMasculine singularFeminine singularMasculine pluralFeminine plural
ioil miola miai mieile mie
tuil tuola tuai tuoile tue
lui/leiil suola suai suoile sue
noiil nostrola nostrai nostrile nostre
voiil vostrola vostrai vostrile vostre
loroil lorola loroi lorole loro
Family exception

With a singular close-family noun, the article is usually omitted: mia madre, tuo fratello, sua sorella. It returns with plural forms and with loro: i miei fratelli, la loro madre.

PART 07

Adjective agreement

Many adjectives ending in -o have four forms. Adjectives ending in -e usually have one singular and one plural form.

TypeMasculine singularFeminine singularMasculine pluralFeminine plural
-oaltoaltaaltialte
-egentilegentilegentiligentili
I miei fratelli sono alti. Le mie sorelle sono gentili.
My brothers are tall. My sisters are kind.

PART 08

Dialogue: introducing your family

ElenaQuesta è la mia famiglia.
This is my family.
PaoloChi è questa?
Who is this?
ElenaÈ mia madre. Si chiama Laura.
She is my mother. Her name is Laura.
PaoloE questi sono i tuoi fratelli?
And are these your brothers?
ElenaSì, sono Marco e Luca. Sono molto simpatici.
Yes, they are Marco and Luca. They are very friendly.

PART 09

Practice and answer key

  1. Translate: my mother.
  2. Translate: my brothers.
  3. Choose: Anna è simpatico / simpatica.
  4. Complete: I miei genitori ___ a Roma (abitare).
  5. Translate: Her sister is tall.
Answers

1. Mia madre. 2. I miei fratelli. 3. Simpatica. 4. Abitano. 5. Sua sorella è alta.

PART 10

Speaking and writing assignment

Describe three people in your family or close circle. Include their relationship to you, name, age, where they live, appearance and personality. Use at least five possessives and six adjectives. Read your description aloud.

Lesson complete

Review the examples, say the Italian phrases aloud and complete the speaking task. Return to any section that still feels difficult. Regular short practice is more effective than memorizing a long list once.
