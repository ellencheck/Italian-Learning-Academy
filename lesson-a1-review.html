PART 01

Learning goals

This chapter checks whether you can manage familiar everyday situations: introduce yourself, describe people, talk about routine, order food, shop and ask for directions.

PART 05

Reading: Una giornata semplice

Mi chiamo Sofia e abito a Bologna. Ho ventotto anni e lavoro in una piccola libreria in centro. Ogni mattina mi sveglio alle sette, faccio colazione e bevo un cappuccino. Alle otto prendo l'autobus. Al lavoro parlo con molti clienti e consiglio libri. A pranzo mangio un'insalata o un panino. Dopo il lavoro incontro un'amica in un bar. La sera torno a casa, preparo la cena e leggo un po'. Mi piace la mia città perché è bella e tranquilla.

Translation: My name is Sofia and I live in Bologna. I am twenty-eight and work in a small bookshop in the centre. Every morning I wake up at seven, have breakfast and drink a cappuccino. At eight I take the bus. At work I speak with many customers and recommend books. At lunch I eat a salad or a sandwich. After work I meet a friend in a café. In the evening I go home, prepare dinner and read a little. I like my city because it is beautiful and peaceful.

PART 06

Reading comprehension

  1. Where does Sofia live?
  2. How old is she?
  3. Where does she work?
  4. What does she drink at breakfast?
  5. How does she travel to work?
  6. What does she do after work?
Answers

1. Bologna. 2. Twenty-eight. 3. In a bookshop. 4. A cappuccino. 5. By bus. 6. She meets a friend in a café.

PART 07

Integrated language practice

  1. Io ___ di Kyiv. (essere)
  2. Noi ___ due sorelle. (avere)
  3. Marco ___ italiano. (parlare)
  4. Voi ___ un libro. (leggere)
  5. Loro ___ presto. (partire)
  6. Mi ___ la pizza. (piacere)
  7. ___ farmacia è vicino alla piazza. (the)
  8. Translate: We would like a table for two.
Answers

1. sono. 2. abbiamo. 3. parla. 4. leggete. 5. partono. 6. piace. 7. La. 8. Vorremmo un tavolo per due.

PART 08

Dialogue challenge: in town

VisitatoreScusi, dov'è la stazione?
Excuse me, where is the station?
ResidenteÈ vicino alla piazza. Vada sempre dritto.
It is near the square. Go straight ahead.
VisitatoreC'è un bar qui vicino?
Is there a café nearby?
ResidenteSì, c'è un bar accanto alla farmacia.
Yes, there is a café next to the pharmacy.
VisitatoreGrazie!
Thank you!
ResidentePrego, buona giornata!
You're welcome, have a nice day!

PART 09

Final writing task

Write 80–100 words about yourself and an ordinary day. Include personal information, family, work or study, food, time and preferences. Use essere, avere, at least eight present-tense verbs, two articles of each type and five time or place expressions.

Mi chiamo ____. Abito a ____. La mattina ____. Nel pomeriggio ____. Mi piace ____ perché ____.
My name is ____. I live in ____. In the morning ____. In the afternoon ____. I like ____ because ____.

PART 10

Final self-assessment

  • I can introduce myself without reading.
  • I can conjugate a regular verb from each group.
  • I can use essere and avere correctly.
  • I can order breakfast and ask the price.
  • I can understand a short A1 text.
  • I can give simple directions.

If one skill is difficult, return to that chapter and practise aloud before moving to A2.

Lesson complete

Review the examples, say the Italian phrases aloud and complete the speaking task. Return to any section that still feels difficult. Regular short practice is more effective than memorizing a long list once.
