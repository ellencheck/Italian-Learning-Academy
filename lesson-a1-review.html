A1 · LESSON 12

A1 Review & Real-Life Communication

Bring together grammar, vocabulary, listening-style reading, translation and conversation

PART 01

What A1 means

At A1 you can understand and use familiar everyday expressions, introduce yourself, ask and answer simple personal questions, and interact when the other person speaks slowly and clearly. This review combines the core topics: pronunciation, greetings, essere, avere, articles, regular present-tense verbs, family, food, time, directions and shopping.

PART 02

Grammar checklist

ESSERE: sono, sei, è, siamo, siete, sono. AVERE: ho, hai, ha, abbiamo, avete, hanno. Articles: il/lo/l'/la and i/gli/le; un/uno/una/un'. Regular present: -are (-o, -i, -a, -iamo, -ate, -ano); -ere (-o, -i, -e, -iamo, -ete, -ono); -ire (-o, -i, -e, -iamo, -ite, -ono); -isc- in four forms for verbs such as capire and finire. Negation: non before the verb. Likes: mi piace + singular/infinitive; mi piacciono + plural.

PART 03

Reading: Una giornata semplice

Mi chiamo Sofia e abito a Bologna. Ho ventotto anni e lavoro in una piccola libreria in centro. Ogni mattina mi sveglio alle sette, faccio colazione e bevo un cappuccino. Alle otto prendo l'autobus. Al lavoro parlo con molti clienti e consiglio libri. A pranzo mangio un'insalata o un panino. Dopo il lavoro incontro un'amica in un bar. Ordiniamo due caffè e parliamo della nostra settimana. La sera torno a casa, preparo la cena e leggo un po'. Mi piace la mia città perché è bella e tranquilla.

PART 04

Reading comprehension

1. Where does Sofia live? 2. How old is she? 3. Where does she work? 4. What does she drink at breakfast? 5. How does she travel to work? 6. What does she do after work? Answers: 1. Bologna. 2. Twenty-eight. 3. In a bookshop in the centre. 4. A cappuccino. 5. By bus. 6. She meets a friend in a café.

PART 05

Integrated grammar practice

Complete: 1. Io ___ di Kyiv. (essere) 2. Noi ___ due sorelle. (avere) 3. Marco ___ italiano. (parlare) 4. Voi ___ un libro. (leggere) 5. Loro ___ presto. (partire) 6. Mi ___ la pizza. (piacere) 7. ___ farmacia è vicino alla piazza. (the) Answers: 1. sono. 2. abbiamo. 3. parla. 4. leggete. 5. partono. 6. piace. 7. La.

PART 06

Real-life scenario: introduce yourself

Say your name, country, city, age, profession, languages and one thing you like. Ask the other person their name, where they live and what languages they speak. Use both an informal and formal version.

PART 07

Real-life scenario: café and directions

You are in a new Italian town. Ask where the nearest café is. At the café, order a drink and snack, ask the price and pay. Then ask for directions to the train station. Include polite expressions and at least one question with dov'è.

PART 08

Final writing task

Write 80–100 words about yourself and an ordinary day. Include personal information, family, work/study, food, daily routine, time and preferences. Use essere, avere, at least eight present-tense verbs, two articles of each type, and at least five time or place expressions.

PART 09

Final self-assessment

Can you introduce yourself without reading? Can you conjugate one regular verb from each group? Can you use essere and avere correctly? Can you order breakfast? Can you ask the price? Can you understand a short A1 text? Can you give simple directions? If one area is difficult, return to that lesson and practise aloud before moving to A2.

Lesson complete

Review the examples, say the Italian phrases aloud and complete the speaking task. Return to any section that still feels difficult. Regular short practice is more effective than memorizing a long list once.
