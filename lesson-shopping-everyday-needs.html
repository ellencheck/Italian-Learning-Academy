Shopping & Everyday Needs

Ask prices, choose products, talk about sizes and quantities, make polite requests and pay in Italian.

PART 01

Learning goals

Practise the language needed in a market, supermarket and clothes shop. You will ask prices, request quantities, choose items and complete a simple purchase.

PART 04

Asking and understanding prices

Question / phraseMeaning
Quanto costa?How much is it? (singular)
Quanto costano?How much are they? (plural)
Quanto viene in tutto?How much is it altogether?
Costa dieci euro.It costs ten euros.
Costano venti euro.They cost twenty euros.
È troppo caro/a.It is too expensive.
Va bene, grazie.That's fine, thank you.

Match the verb to the item: costa for one item, costano for multiple items.

PART 05

Polite requests

Vorrei (“I would like”) is a polite and useful way to ask for something. Posso…? asks “May I…?” or “Can I…?”

Vorrei mezzo chilo di mele, per favore.
I would like half a kilo of apples, please.
Posso provare questa camicia?
May I try on this shirt?
Posso pagare con carta?
Can I pay by card?
Sto solo guardando, grazie.
I'm just looking, thank you.

PART 06

Questo and quello

Questo means this; quello means that. They agree with the noun in gender and number.

FormExampleMeaning
questoquesto librothis book
questaquesta borsathis bag
questiquesti pantalonithese trousers
questequeste scarpethese shoes
quelquel negoziothat shop
quellaquella casathat house
quegliquegli studentithose students
quellequelle borsethose bags

Quello changes according to rules similar to the definite article: quel, quello, quell', quei, quegli, quelle.

PART 08

Dialogue: at the market

ClienteBuongiorno, quanto costano i pomodori?
Good morning, how much are the tomatoes?
VenditoreTre euro al chilo.
Three euros per kilo.
ClienteMe ne dà mezzo chilo, per favore?
Could you give me half a kilo, please?
VenditoreCerto. Altro?
Of course. Anything else?
ClienteUn chilo di mele. Quanto viene in tutto?
A kilo of apples. How much is it altogether?
VenditoreQuattro euro e cinquanta.
Four euros fifty.

PART 09

Dialogue: in a clothes shop

ClientePosso provare questa camicia?
May I try on this shirt?
CommessaCerto, il camerino è a destra.
Of course, the fitting room is on the right.
ClienteAvete una taglia più piccola?
Do you have a smaller size?
CommessaSì, ecco una taglia 40.
Yes, here is a size 40.
ClientePerfetto. Quanto costa?
Perfect. How much is it?
CommessaCosta trentacinque euro.
It costs thirty-five euros.

PART 10

Practice and answer key

  1. How much does it cost? (one item)
  2. How much do they cost? (plural)
  3. Translate: this bag.
  4. Translate: those shoes.
  5. I would like half a kilo of apples.
  6. May I pay by card?
Answers

1. Quanto costa? 2. Quanto costano? 3. Questa borsa. 4. Quelle scarpe. 5. Vorrei mezzo chilo di mele. 6. Posso pagare con carta?

PART 11

Speaking assignment

Role-play buying fruit at a market and a shirt in a clothes shop. Ask three questions, use two quantities, ask about a size, state a colour preference and complete the payment politely.

Lesson complete

Review the examples, say the Italian phrases aloud and complete the speaking task. Return to any section that still feels difficult. Regular short practice is more effective than memorizing a long list once.
