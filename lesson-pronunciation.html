Italian Sounds & Spelling

Learn to read Italian confidently: vowels, consonants, spelling patterns, syllables and word stress.

PART 01

Learning goals

By the end, you can pronounce familiar Italian words, spell a name, recognize the main sound patterns and understand why vowel clarity and double consonants matter.

  • Say the five vowels clearly.
  • Distinguish hard and soft C/G.
  • Read CH, GH, GN, GLI, SC and QU.
  • Hear double consonants and identify word stress.

PART 02

The Italian alphabet

Italian traditionally uses 21 letters. J, K, W, X and Y appear mostly in foreign names and borrowed words. Letter names are useful when spelling.

PART 03

The five vowel sounds

Italian vowels stay clear, even when they are unstressed. Do not reduce them to a weak neutral sound as English often does.

VowelPronunciation guideExamples
Aopen ahcasa, pasta
Eclear eh / closed ebene, perché
Ieevino, libri
Oopen aw / closed osole, notte
Uooluna, tutto
La casa è bella. Il vino è buono.
The house is beautiful. The wine is good.
Keep the final vowels audible.

PART 04

C and G: hard and soft sounds

C and G change sound before E and I. H keeps them hard.

SpellingSoundExamples
CA, CO, CUhard kcasa, cosa, cucina
CE, CIsoft chcena, cinema
CHE, CHIhard kche, chi
GA, GO, GUhard ggatto, gomma, gusto
GE, GIsoft jgelato, giro
GHE, GHIhard gspaghetti, laghi
Remember

C + E/I is soft. CH + E/I is hard. G + E/I is soft. GH + E/I is hard.

Practise GLI and GN slowly before saying the whole sentence.

PART 06

Double consonants and syllables

A double consonant is held longer. It can distinguish words with different meanings. Give the consonant a small pause, then release it.

Single consonantDouble consonantMeaning
palapallashovel / ball
casacassahouse / box or till
nonononnoninth / grandfather
setesettethirst / seven

Clap syllables: ra-gaz-zo, ge-la-to, fa-mi-glia, te-le-fo-no. This helps you hear the rhythm.

PART 07

Word stress and written accents

Many Italian words are stressed on the second-to-last syllable: ra-GAZ-zo, ge-LA-to, a-MI-co. Others are stressed earlier: TA-vo-lo, TE-le-fo-no. A written accent often marks stress on the final syllable.

WordStressMeaning
cittàcit-TÀcity
caffècaf-FÈcoffee
perchéper-CHÉwhy / because

PART 08

Guided pronunciation practice

Read each group slowly, then at natural speed. Repeat three times and record yourself if possible.

casa · cena · chi · cucina · gelato · spaghetti
house · dinner · who/what · kitchen · ice cream · spaghetti
famiglia · figlio · pesce · scena · quattro · città
family · son · fish · scene · four · city
Three-step practice

First listen to or read the word. Second, pronounce it slowly. Third, use it in a complete sentence.

PART 09

Common mistakes

  • Pronouncing cena with a hard k instead of a soft ch.
  • Pronouncing chi like English “chee”; it begins with a hard k.
  • Dropping the final vowel in grazie.
  • Ignoring the length of double consonants.
  • Moving the stress to the wrong syllable.
Self-correction

Compare casa / cassa and pala / palla. If the two words sound identical, practise the double consonant again.

PART 10

Practice and answer key

  1. Which spelling gives a hard k before i: ci or chi?
  2. Which combination sounds like sh before e: sce or sche?
  3. What changes between pala and palla?
  4. Where is the stress in tavolo?
  5. Spell the word Roma using Italian letter names.
Answers

1. Chi. 2. Sce. 3. The double l is longer and changes the word. 4. First syllable: TA-vo-lo. 5. Erre — o — emme — a.

PART 11

Speaking assignment

Spell your first and last name in Italian. Then introduce yourself in three sentences. Include your name, where you live and one thing you like. Read slowly once, then naturally. Pay attention to vowels, double consonants and stress.

Mi chiamo ____. Abito a ____. Mi piace la lingua italiana.
My name is ____. I live in ____. I like the Italian language.

Lesson complete

Review the examples, say the Italian phrases aloud and complete the speaking task. Return to any section that still feels difficult. Regular short practice is more effective than memorizing a long list once.
